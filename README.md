# E-Commerce Sales & Operational Performance Analysis

An end-to-end business analytics project that uses **Excel, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze e-commerce sales, profitability, regional performance, and operational efficiency.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow — from raw data cleaning and database preparation to SQL analysis and interactive Power BI reporting.

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data to understand:

- Sales and revenue trends
- Product and sub-category performance
- Profitability
- Regional sales performance
- Sales channel performance
- Order status and delivery performance
- Monthly sales and profit trends

The raw transactional dataset was first inspected and cleaned in Excel, then loaded into PostgreSQL for structured storage and SQL-based analysis. The analyzed data was finally used to build an interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis focuses on the following business questions:

1. What are the main revenue drivers across products, sub-categories, and sales channels?
2. Which products contribute most to sales and profitability?
3. How does profitability vary across different regions and product segments?
4. Are there regional differences in delivery performance?
5. What is the monthly trend of sales and profit?
6. How do sales channels differ in terms of sales and order volume?
7. What is the distribution of orders across different order statuses?
